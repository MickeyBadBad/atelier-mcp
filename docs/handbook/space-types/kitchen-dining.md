# Space Type: Kitchen + Dining (厨房 + 餐厅)

> Sources: Neufert *Architects' Data* (4th International English ed., Internet Archive PDF, kitchen + restaurant chapters); NKBA *Kitchen Planning Guidelines with Access Standards* (National Kitchen & Bath Association, 2022 ed., publicly hosted PDF); Time-Saver Standards for Building Types (McGraw-Hill, restaurant / kitchen sections, p. 755 ff., archived on arc213.files.wordpress.com); GB 50096-2011 《住宅设计规范》§5.3 (residential kitchen) (jianbiaoku reproduction; cross-ref `codes.md`); GB 50034-2013 《建筑照明设计标准》§5.1 Table 5.1.1 (Wosen LED / Recolux summaries; cross-ref `lighting.md`); Layak Architect "Kitchen Dimensions" citing Neufert / Metric Handbook / Time-Saver / Indian National Building Code 2016; CRD Design Build "Kitchen Dimensions: Code Requirements & NKBA Guidelines" reproducing the NKBA 31 numbered guidelines; Dimensions.com "Dining Room Clearances".
> Last updated: 2026-04-29

## Purpose

The kitchen + dining envelope (厨房 + 餐厅) handles food preparation, cooking, and seated meals. Unlike the bedroom or living-room chapters this one covers **two adjacent functional zones** because their clearances interlock: the kitchen aisle and the dining chair pull-out share a single circulation band in most apartments. Downstream consumers are the `interior-discovery-intake` and `interior-plain-language-edit` skills plus the 9-dimension quality gates.

## Furniture inventory

### Must-have (kitchen)

- **Sink + cooktop + refrigerator** forming the work triangle — total leg length 3.6–6.6 m, no leg < 1.2 m or > 2.7 m (per Layak Architect "Kitchen Dimensions" citing Neufert / Metric Handbook; corroborated by NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines #3 on CRD Design Build).
- **Continuous counter run** with the 600 mm depth and 850–950 mm height per row (per Layak Architect / Neufert kitchen counter band).
- **Upper cabinets or open shelving** in the 750–1500 mm comfortable storage band above counter (per Layak Architect / Neufert vertical-dimensions block).
- **Range hood / ventilation** — code-mandatory in residential cooking zones (per GB 50096-2011 §6.7 ventilation clauses summarised in `codes.md`; specific clause not reproduced here).

### Must-have (dining)

- **Dining table** sized to occupant count — see standard footprints above.
- **Dining chairs** per occupant count, with per-person 600 mm minimum and 660–760 mm preferred edge allocation (per Neufert restaurant chapter; Dimensions.com "Dining Room Clearances").
- **Pendant or downlights centred over the table** delivering the GB 50034-2013 dining task target — see lighting recipe below.

### Common

- **Kitchen island or peninsula** (kitchens ≥ 6 m² with 1500 mm clear surrounding aisle) — Time-Saver Standards / NKBA acceptable layout (per Layak Architect "Kitchen Dimensions" citing Time-Saver Standards on galley / L / U / island typologies; corroborated by NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines #4 on CRD Design Build: "a full-height, full-depth, tall obstacle should not separate two primary work centers").
- **Counter-height seating** (760 mm seat for a 900 mm counter, or 660 mm seat for an 870 mm bar overhang) — per-person 610 mm wide (per NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines #9; CRD Design Build).
- **Sideboard / buffet** in the dining zone for plate storage and serving — Time-Saver Standards convention; not regulated by code.

### Optional

- **Eat-in dinette** combined into a kitchen ≥ 7.5 m² (per Layak Architect "Kitchen Dimensions" dining-kitchen 7.5 m² floor) — drops the standalone dining room.
- **Wine fridge / second fridge** when the kitchen exceeds 8 m² and the work triangle still holds (per NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines #3 — additional appliance legs must still each measure 4–9 ft / 1.2–2.7 m).
- **Bar / coffee station** as a dedicated counter sub-zone — adds a fourth point to the work triangle which the NKBA #3 rule explicitly handles ("each additional travel distance to another appliance/work center should measure no less than 4 ft nor more than 9 ft").

## Lighting layer recipe

| Layer | Target (lx) | Kelvin | Fixture | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ambient (kitchen) | 100 lx maintained on a 0.75 m horizontal plane | 2700–3500 K | Overhead fixture or recessed downlights | (per GB 50034-2013 §5.1 Table 5.1.1 row "厨房 一般活动" 100 lx, 0.75 m horizontal plane, Ra ≥ 80, as summarised on Wosen LED / Recolux Lighting; cross-ref `lighting.md`) |
| Task (kitchen counter) | 500–750 lx maintained at the counter | 3000–4000 K (closer to neutral for color judgement of food) | Under-cabinet linear LED, focused downlights over sink/range | (per GB 50034-2013 §5.1 Table 5.1.1 row "厨房 操作台" 500 lx as summarised on Wosen LED / Recolux Lighting; corroborated by Super Bright LEDs IES residential summary "kitchen task 70 fc ≈ 750 lux"; cross-ref `lighting.md`) |
| Ambient (dining) | 150 lx maintained at the table top | 2700–3000 K (residential) / 2400–2700 K (intimate / fine dining) | Pendant centred over table, 750–900 mm above table top | (per GB 50034-2013 §5.1 Table 5.1.1 row "餐厅" 150 lx, table-top reference plane, Ra ≥ 80, as summarised on Wosen LED / Recolux Lighting; cross-ref `lighting.md` Restaurant rows) |
| Decorative (dining) | not metered | 2200–2700 K | Picture light, sconce, candles | (per `lighting.md` decorative-layer cross-ref; warmer than the ambient/task layers for atmosphere) |

Operationalising the recipe:
- The kitchen ambient and task layers together must satisfy the GB 50034-2013 floor — ambient alone is well below the counter target (100 lx vs. 500 lx). Trips the "Lighting ≥ 2 layers" quality gate before render (per workflow spec § Quality Gates #2).
- The dining pendant should hang **750–900 mm above the table top** so the bottom of the shade sits below seated eye level — keeps the 150 lx task target tight on the table rather than spilling onto the floor. Standard residential convention; not directly regulated by GB 50034 but corroborated by Layak Architect dining lighting guidance.
- Mix Kelvin **across** layers, not within. Cooler-neutral 3500–4000 K for the kitchen task light (color judgement) is acceptable next to a 2700–3000 K dining pendant when the two zones are visually separated by ≥ 1.5 m (per `lighting.md` Kelvin-coherence rule).

## Worked examples

**2.4 m × 3.0 m galley kitchen (7.2 m²) + adjacent 2.4 m × 3.5 m dining (8.4 m²), urban apartment.**
Kitchen usable area = 7.2 m² → above the GB 50096-2011 §5.3.1 4.0 m² floor, comfortably inside Layak Architect "standard kitchen" 6 m² band (per Layak Architect "Kitchen Dimensions"). Single-row counter on the 3.0 m wall, opposite wall holds upper-cabinet-only with appliances tucked into the run. Aisle = **2400 − 600 (counter depth) = 1800 mm** — clears both the GB 50096-2011 §5.3.5 1500 mm single-row floor and the NKBA #6 1067 mm work-aisle floor (per NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines on CRD Design Build). Work triangle: refrigerator at one end, range mid-run, sink at the far end, total ≈ 4.5 m — inside the 3.6–6.6 m band (per Layak Architect; NKBA #3). Dining: 4-seat rectangular table 1200 × 750 mm centred in the 8.4 m² dining zone. Table-to-wall both long sides = **(2400 − 750) ÷ 2 = 825 mm** — clears Neufert 750 mm seated-only band (per Neufert restaurant chapter); end-to-wall = **(3500 − 1200) ÷ 2 = 1150 mm** — supports walking-behind use (per Neufert 1000 mm walking-behind band). Hero camera at 24 mm height 1.55 m from the entry corner; pendant centred over the table at 800 mm above table top sized for 150 lx (per GB 50034-2013 §5.1 Table 5.1.1 餐厅).

**3.6 m × 3.6 m eat-in kitchen (12.96 m²), island layout.**
Above the GB 50096-2011 §5.3.1 4.0 m² floor and the Layak Architect 7.5 m² dining-kitchen floor (per Layak Architect "Kitchen Dimensions"). Perimeter L-counter on two walls, freestanding **1500 × 900 mm island** centred. Island clear zone all sides = **(3600 − 1500) ÷ 2 = 1050 mm** at the long end, **(3600 − 900) ÷ 2 = 1350 mm** at the short — multi-cook NKBA #6 1219 mm floor met on the short axis; on the long axis we sit just below the multi-cook 1219 mm but above the single-cook 1067 mm (per NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines on CRD Design Build), so flag the layout as single-cook only. Counter seating along the long side of the island for 3 stools at 610 mm each = 1830 mm overhang — inside the 1500 mm island length, so cap at **2 stools × 610 mm = 1220 mm**. Dining table demoted to optional in this layout.

## Common mistakes

- **Aisle below the GB 50096-2011 1500 mm single-row floor.** Common in narrow galley apartments — the §5.3.5 number is mandatory, not advisory (per GB 50096-2011 §5.3.5; cross-ref `codes.md`). If the structural shell forces ≤ 1500 mm, the design either drops the kitchen function from the room (becomes a tea-counter) or files for a §5.3.5 deviation with the AHJ.
- **Work-triangle leg < 1.2 m or > 2.7 m.** A leg below 1.2 m collides counter zones (no landing area between sink and range — fails NKBA #11 24-in landing); a leg above 2.7 m converts the triangle into a corridor and slows the cook (per NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines #3 on CRD Design Build).
- **Dining pendant too high / too low.** Above ~ 900 mm over the table the lux drops and the shade reads as ceiling-clutter; below ~ 700 mm it sits in the eye line of seated diners. Lock to 750–900 mm above table top (per Layak Architect dining-lighting convention; `lighting.md` cross-ref).
- **6-person table in a 10 m² dining zone with seated-only clearances.** Even at the Neufert 750 mm seated band, 6 people need walk-around access for service. Push to the 1000 mm walking-behind band on at least two sides (per Neufert restaurant chapter); otherwise downgrade to 4-seat.
- **Skipping the kitchen task layer.** Ambient alone delivers 100 lx — well below the GB 50034-2013 §5.1 Table 5.1.1 厨房 操作台 500 lx target. Trips the "Lighting ≥ 2 layers" quality gate (per workflow spec § Quality Gates #2).
